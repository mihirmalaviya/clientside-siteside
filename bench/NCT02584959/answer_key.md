# NCT02584959 — Shire ViroPharma SHP616-300, Phase 3 (SC C1-INH, HAE), 3-sequence crossover

- BEFORE (input to checker): Original Protocol v1.0, 28 May 2015 — https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/59/NCT02584959/Prot_000.pdf
- AFTER (source of key): Amendment 1 (v2.0), 22 Jul 2015 — https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/59/NCT02584959/Prot_001.pdf (Summary of Changes, PDF p3–4)
- Note: the sponsor's table omits "minor editorial revisions (including changes for consistency and clarity)", so this key undercounts real fixes.

## In-scope avoidable fixes (verified present in BEFORE text)

| ID | BEFORE text | AFTER change / sponsor wording | Check type |
|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | Secondary endpoint (synopsis + §9.8.2.2): "Disease activity as measured by the 196-day AAS… sum of the daily AAS during a treatment period", but "Each treatment period will be 14 weeks" (= 98 days) | "Corrected the secondary efficacy endpoint for the AAS, to indicate the '98-day AAS' will be calculated in each treatment period" | Number inconsistent with design |
| K2 | §9.8.1: "The primary efficacy analysis will be conducted over the Full Analysis Set" (C1-INH vs placebo), while sequence 3 (A/A, ~22 subjects) never receives placebo | "Clarified the population of subjects (ie, those randomized to Treatment Sequence 1 [A/B] or 2 [B/A]) to be used in the evaluation of the efficacy… compared to placebo" (§9.8.1, §9.8.2.2) | Analysis population inconsistent with design |
| K3 (soft) | AE period mostly "through 7 days after the last dose", but one statement: "collected from the time informed consent is signed (Section 8)" with no end | "Clarified the collection period for AEs" (§8.1) | Same period stated differently |

## Excluded (design/strategy/safety-data driven)
Multicenter title; attack-rate threshold ≥4→≥3 and the 25% ≥4-attacks requirement; parent/caregiver administration and diary help; SHP616-103 data updates; PK presentation/dose rationale; icatibant guidance; two-phase randomization; removal of antibody-titer follow-up; removal of photographs of injection-site reactions.
