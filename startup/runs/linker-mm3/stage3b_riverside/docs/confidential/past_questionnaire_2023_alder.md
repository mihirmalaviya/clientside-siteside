# Site Feasibility Questionnaire: Alder Therapeutics (completed 2023-04-18)
Site: Riverside Cancer Center, Dayton, Ohio. Completed by: Maya Chen, Lead CRC

Q: Number of clinical research coordinators (CRCs) on the hematology research team?
A: 2

Q: Does your site have inpatient hematology beds?
A: No. Step-up dosing is done outpatient with 24-hour observation in the infusion center.

Q: Does your site have an intensive care unit?
A: Yes, on campus (Riverside Medical Center).

Q: Number of -80C freezers available for research samples?
A: 1

Q: Typical IRB approval time?
A: 8 weeks (Riverside IRB, local)

Q: Can the site use a central IRB?
A: Yes, WCG IRB.

Q: Annual number of relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma (RRMM) patients seen?
A: ~50

Q: Is PET-CT available?
A: Referral to Valley Imaging Partners.
