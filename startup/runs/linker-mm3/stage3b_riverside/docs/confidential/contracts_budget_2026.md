# Riverside Cancer Research Office: contracts and budget reference (internal, 2026)

- Indirect cost (overhead) rate for industry trials: 29%, effective 2026-07-01 (FY2027). Previous rate 28% (FY2025-26).
- IRB: local Riverside IRB (median 6 weeks to approval) or WCG central IRB; both accepted.
- Contract and budget milestones are logged per trial in ops_log.csv (final protocol received → CTA and budget executed).
- Startup fee: $4,500 non-refundable. Pharmacy setup: $2,000. IRB fee (local): $3,000.
- Other sponsors' budgets and terms are confidential and are never shared.
