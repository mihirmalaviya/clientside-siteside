# Sources

Method: scored US sites across prior trials via ctg.py (Janssen MM, all daratumumab, MM+ASCT, MM+lenalidomide maintenance, MM+MRD) plus history.csv, weighted by recency and Janssen sponsorship; Janssen trials with anonymized sites (notably GRIFFIN NCT02874742, a Janssen dara-RVd transplant/maintenance P2, 36 US cities) were mapped by city to the leading transplant center there.

## Top 10 reasons
1. Dana-Farber: highest score overall; on Janssen AQUILA/dara trials and most MM transplant/maintenance trials.
2. MSKCC: second-highest score; heavy dara/MRD investigator-initiated work, GRIFFIN city.
3. Mayo Clinic Rochester: top-3 MM site, on Janssen AQUILA and dara AL trials, big ASCT volume.
4. Levine Cancer Institute: on GRIFFIN-city, AQUILA, ANDROMEDA, CEPHEUS; top Janssen partner.
5. WashU St Louis: GRIFFIN city, AQUILA, ANDROMEDA; large ASCT program.
6. MD Anderson: GRIFFIN city, CEPHEUS, ANDROMEDA; large myeloma/transplant dept.
7. City of Hope: GRIFFIN city, ANDROMEDA, Janssen dara P1; large BMT program.
8. Hackensack: high MM trial count, big myeloma/transplant division.
9. Colorado Blood Cancer Institute: high MM industry score, ANDROMEDA, transplant center.
10. Emory Winship: GRIFFIN city, AQUILA, ANDROMEDA; major ASCT program.
