# Sources

Method: I scored each site on its history.csv trial rows, weighted toward recent trials. Points came from: Regeneron trials (LINKER-MM1 NCT03761108, NCT05137054), industry sponsorship, bispecific/CAR-T (CRS-capable) trials, RRMM, P3, and pom/elotuzumab regimens.

## Top 10
1. 3274 MSK New York: highest volume of recent industry RRMM, bispecific, and CAR-T trials in history.
2. 1033 Dana-Farber: second-highest volume of bispecific/CAR-T RRMM trials. Its Harvard listing ran Regeneron NCT05137054.
3. 2245 Levine Cancer Institute: heavy recent industry myeloma P3 and bispecific activity.
4. 3903 Ohio State James: ran both Regeneron linvoseltamab trials. Bumma (OSU) is first author of LINKER-MM1.
5. 3073 Mayo Clinic Rochester: Regeneron NCT05137054 site and a major bispecific/CAR-T center.
6. 3188 Medical College of Wisconsin: high recent industry RRMM and cellular-therapy volume.
7. 2150 Karmanos: Regeneron NCT05137054 site. Zonder (Karmanos) is a LINKER-MM1 author.
8. 886 Colorado Blood Cancer Institute: frequent industry RRMM and bispecific trial site.
9. 3160 MD Anderson: high RRMM industry volume. Hans Lee (MDA) is a LINKER-MM1 investigator.
10. 5929 WashU St Louis: high recent industry bispecific/CAR-T RRMM volume.
