# SOP HEM-RES-014: Management of CRS and ICANS for T-cell Engaging Therapies
Version 2.0, effective 2025-03-01. Supersedes version 1.0 (2023).

## Summary of changes from v1.0
- Step-up dosing now performed INPATIENT on 6 West oncology unit. Four (4) beds are reserved for research step-up dosing admissions.
- Tocilizumab: the investigational/inpatient pharmacy keeps a minimum of 2 doses per enrolled patient on site at all times.

## Procedures
- CRS grading per ASTCT consensus; ICE score assessed by trained nursing every 12 hours during step-up dosing.
- 24/7 hematology attending and fellow on call; neurology consult available 24/7.
- ICU transfer pathway for grade 3+ CRS or ICANS (ICU in same building).
