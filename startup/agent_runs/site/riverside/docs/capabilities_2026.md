# Riverside Cancer Center: Research Capabilities (updated 2026-02)

## Facilities
- Intensive care unit: Riverside Medical Center ICU, 24 beds, same building as the cancer center, 24/7 intensivist coverage.
- Inpatient oncology unit: 6 West (see CRS/ICANS SOP v2 for reserved research beds).
- Infusion center: 18 chairs.

## Laboratory and samples
- Clinical laboratory: CLIA certified (CLIA #36D9999999), CAP accredited.
- -80C freezers: 2 (continuous temperature monitoring with alarm and backup power).
- -20C freezer: 1. Refrigerated centrifuge: yes.
- Bone marrow aspirate and biopsy: performed on site in the hematology clinic.
- MRD testing by next-generation sequencing: not performed locally (send-out to central/sponsor lab).

## Imaging
- MRI (1.5T): on site. CT: on site.
- PET-CT: not on site. Referral to Valley Imaging Partners (average 5 business days to schedule).

## Other
- Apheresis: not on site; referral to Miami Valley Apheresis Center.
- Investigational drug service: 2-8C and controlled room temperature storage, continuous temperature logging.
- EMR: Epic.
