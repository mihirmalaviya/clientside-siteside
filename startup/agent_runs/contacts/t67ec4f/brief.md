# New trial (sponsor's view)

- Sponsor: K36 Therapeutics, Inc.
- Phase 1, adults with relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma
- Arms: Cohort A1 & A2: KTX-1001; Cohort B1 & B2: KTX-1001+Mezigdomide; Cohort C1 & C2: KTX-1001 + Carfilzomib (KYPROLIS®); Cohort D: KTX-1001+ pomalidomide (Pomalyst, Imnovid)

## Summary
A Phase I study to evaluate the safety of a novel, orally available, selective, and potent small molecule inhibitor of the histone lysine methyl transferase MMSET (also known as NSD2/WHSC1) to prevent the dimethylation of H3K36 in adult patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma (RRMM).
