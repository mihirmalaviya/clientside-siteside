# Riverside Cancer Center: Research Capabilities (updated 2026-09, supersedes 2026-02)

## Facilities
- Intensive care unit: Riverside Medical Center ICU, 24 beds, same building as the cancer center, 24/7 intensivist coverage.
- Inpatient oncology unit: 6 West. 4 beds reserved for research step-up dosing admissions (SOP HEM-RES-014 v2).
- 6 West nursing: 24 nurses, 22 with current ICE/ICANS assessment training (annual competency, last cycle 2026-08). Only ICE-trained nurses are assigned to step-up dosing patients.
- Infusion center: 18 chairs (expanded from 16 in 2026-06).

## Laboratory and samples
- Clinical laboratory: CLIA certified (CLIA #36D9999999), CAP accredited.
- -80C freezers: 2 (continuous temperature monitoring, alarm, backup power). -20C freezer: 1. Refrigerated centrifuge.
- Bone marrow aspirate and biopsy: on site, hematology clinic.
- Shipping: fresh bone marrow aspirate to central labs by FedEx Priority Overnight, pickup 16:30 Monday-Thursday. Friday collections cannot ship same day. Packed by IATA-certified coordinators.
- MRD testing by next-generation sequencing: not performed locally (send-out).

## Imaging and cardiac
- MRI (1.5T) and CT on site. Echocardiogram and ECG on site.
- PET-CT: not on site. Referral to Valley Imaging Partners (average 5 business days).

## Pharmacy
- Investigational drug service (Dana Wolfe, dedicated pharmacist). 2-8C and room-temperature storage, continuous logging.
- Pomalidomide REMS: pharmacy certified.
- Tocilizumab: at least 2 doses per enrolled T-cell-engager patient kept on site (SOP v2). Inpatient pharmacy open 24/7, so tocilizumab can be given within 2 hours of a CRS diagnosis at any hour.

## Other
- Apheresis: not on site; referral to Valley Apheresis Partners.
- Electronic PRO: staff hand out and collect sponsor-provided tablets at clinic visits (done on HLV-330). Guest Wi-Fi in clinic.
- EMR: Epic, with remote read-only access for monitors. EDC experience: Medidata Rave, Veeva Vault EDC.
- No on-site stem cell transplant program (referral). Not FACT accredited.
