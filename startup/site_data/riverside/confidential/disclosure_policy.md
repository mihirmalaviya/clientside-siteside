# Riverside Cancer Research Office: what we share in feasibility responses

Share with sponsors:
- Aggregate patient numbers (counts, percentages), capabilities, our own timelines, rates and fees.

Never share:
- Anything about an individual patient: MRN, initials, age, visit dates, regimens or notes of a specific person.
- Pipeline trials under confidentiality agreement (CDA): we may say how many are planned, never the sponsor, drug or trial code.
- Other sponsors' budgets, contract terms or unpublished protocol details.
- Staff personal contact details.

When unsure, leave the answer for the research office director to review.
