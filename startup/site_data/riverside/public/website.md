# Riverside Cancer Center (riverside-cancer.example)
_Fictional hospital for a demo. Page text as of 2025-11._

## About
Riverside Cancer Center is the cancer program of Riverside Medical Center in Dayton, Ohio. Our hematology team treats lymphoma, leukemia and multiple myeloma, led by Dr. Elena Marsh (Myeloma Program Lead).

## Services
- Infusion center with 16 chairs
- Inpatient oncology unit (6 West) and an ICU in the same building
- On-site MRI, CT, echocardiography and bone marrow procedures
- Accredited by the Commission on Cancer. Laboratory CAP accredited.

## For sponsors: Clinical Trials Office
The Riverside Cancer Research Office handles feasibility, contracts, budgets and regulatory startup for industry trials. We accept local Riverside IRB or WCG central IRB review. To send a feasibility questionnaire, use the sponsor intake form on this page.

## For patients and families
Patients traveling for treatment can stay at the Riverside Inn, our partner hotel 10 minutes away, at a discounted rate.
